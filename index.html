How Japanese Export Works

From selecting your vehicle in Tokyo to customs clearance at your destination port, we handle everything.

01

Source or Request

Choose from our ready-to-ship stock or tell us your target spec. We search USS Tokyo, TAA, CAA, & JU daily.

02

Physical Inspection

Our certified bilingual mechanics inspect the engine, undercarriage, odometer, and bodywork before bidding.

03

Japanese Deregistration

We purchase the car, process official export certificates through the Ministry of Land & Transport (MLIT).

04

Secure Global Freight

Loaded onto scheduled RoRo vessels or secured in containers with full tracking and insurance till delivery.

Japanese Standard of Integrity

Zero Guesswork. Full Documentation.

Unlike domestic used markets, every Japanese car passes through standardized governmental auctions where mileage tampering or undisclosed accident damage is strictly illegal.

Original Japanese Auction Sheet Translation

We provide accurate English translations of official auction notes, highlighting scratches, panel replacements, and interior marks.

Certified Pre-Shipment Inspections

High-Resolution Yard Photography
